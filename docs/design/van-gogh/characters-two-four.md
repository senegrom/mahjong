# Night Cafe 2 and Cypress Fields 4

Both tiles are approved for deployment in the Van Gogh set. Their source images were already saved in this repository; no image generation or repainting was used for this integration.

- **2 of characters (Characters B):** [Night Cafe](studies/07-two-characters-night-cafe.png).
- **4 of characters (Characters C):** [Cypress Fields](studies/08-four-characters-cypress-fields.png).

The production PNGs are byte-identical to these full-canvas sources. The self-contained SVG wrappers use the existing 300 x 400 canvas, rounded clipping and bleed. All twelve previously approved faces, including Almond Branches 3, East A and North B, are unchanged. The set now has fourteen approved faces and twenty Classic fallbacks. The export manifest records the source and production SHA-256 hashes.
