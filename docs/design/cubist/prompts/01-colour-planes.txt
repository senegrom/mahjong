Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: an elegant art-direction contact sheet for a new mahjong tile set named "Cubist".
Primary request: create six original Cubist mahjong tile face concepts. The existing game has bold, full-bleed Matisse cut-paper faces; preserve their readable scale, generous shapes and flat portrait 3:4 format while inventing an unmistakably CUBIST artistic language.

Make a polished nearly square contact sheet: 3 equal columns, 2 rows, six large upright 3:4 rectangular tile faces, evenly spaced on a warm grey-white page. Discreet heading "CUBIST" in refined black typography at top; subtitle "01 / COLOUR PLANES". Small typeset captions BELOW each face, outside the artwork. Face backgrounds reach every edge. No ivory rims, no physical tile mockup, no bevels, no shadows, no angled perspective of the cards.

Art direction: expressive synthetic cubist painting and papier-colle, angular overlapping planes, dislocated but coherent contours, shifts between frontal and profile views, rhythmic charcoal line fragments, matt pigment and restrained paper grain. Big confident shapes with a few internal facets. Use ochre, parchment, oxblood red, dusty cobalt and emerald, with purposeful dark contours. Rich, sophisticated colour rather than brown monotone. Avoid Matisse's organic fronds, vector low-poly triangles, stained-glass mosaics, photorealistic gemstones, fractals, visual clutter and gratuitous geometric confetti. Each tile should feel like a tiny cubist painting while remaining playable.

Six exact tiles, left-to-right, top then bottom:
A. Caption "A — ONE DISK". One very large circular coin seen in simultaneous front and tilted views, a clearly singular closed outer circle with angular offset internal rings and an off-centre dark aperture. Ochre, indigo, oxblood, ivory on an ochre-and-parchment fractured background. It must read as ONE disk, not several circles.
B. Caption "B — FIVE DISKS". EXACTLY FIVE distinct round disks in a clear quincunx: upper left, upper right, larger centre, lower left, lower right. Each whole disk has a single visible closed outer contour and two or three angular coloured internal planes, with offset dark centres. Strong ochre, cobalt, vermilion on muted light cream. Generous gaps; no sixth decorative disk.
C. Caption "C — ONE BAMBOO". ONE charismatic cubist bird with an angular profile beak, one eye, a tilted faceted wing, broad fan-shaped tail and two feet. Simultaneous frontal and profile body perspectives, like painted collaged sculpture. Ochre head, ivory and blue angular torso, one rust-red wing, near-black contours against forest green. It is a bird, not a human face.
D. Caption "D — TWO BAMBOO". EXACTLY TWO separate jointed bamboo stalks, staggered diagonally, one tall and one short. Strong architectural silhouettes built from two broad faceted segments and one small angled leaf each. Everything INSIDE this face, including background and outlines, MUST be green: dark forest, bottle green, emerald, muted sage, pale mint. No cream, black, yellow, red or blue. Two readable vertical plants with broad open space between them.
E. Caption "E — EIGHT CHARACTERS". Correct readable Chinese characters 八 and 萬, reinterpreted in angular painted cubist strokes and interlocking planes. Upper character 八 is EXACTLY TWO broad separate splayed diagonal strokes, ivory and ochre. Lower 萬 is a recognisable deep red angular glyph with proper structure. Black and dusty blue collage planes through a parchment ground. Do not use Arabic 8, do not mirror or invent glyphs, do not turn this into a portrait.
F. Caption "F — RED DRAGON". A single oversized unmistakable 中 built from a broad red asymmetric rectangular enclosure and one thick vertical red stem passing through its centre and extending above and below. Two large clean internal openings. Displace a few surfaces in cubist perspective, use red and oxblood with ochre and cream background planes and restrained charcoal accents. Preserve the essential 中 structure; no dragon animal illustration.

All six tiles share a sophisticated cohesive Cubist collection identity but have varied composition and different dominant colours. No Latin labels, suit letters or numbers inside faces. The sheet is design exploration, not a diagram. Crisp countable silhouettes, high quality painted surfaces, useful for choosing a direction before production.
